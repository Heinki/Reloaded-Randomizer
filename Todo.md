Hey, not sure where to put it, so I put my feedback to my C&C Reloaded Randomizer run here. I played Shop mode in V 1.0. It was a very fun game mode, surely will play more of it. Thanks for making it. There were only some minor things that I encountered.
Paradropped GIs do not get their full upgrades, tested in Suntemple (Allied9): I bought veteran and stealth upgrade for GIs. The paratropped units were veteran but NOT stealthed. In the vanilla version the units were not veteran. GIs trained in other missions had both upgrades. Also the rescued GIs had no upgrades, but this is intended, when I understand it correctly.
Warfactory was not buildable with tanks unlocked (Rhino, Titan, Gatling), tested in Nod1. Not a big issue as this will only affact few and easy missions but surely not what was intended.
Some buildings have no requirements. Helipad (only Nod tested) was builable right away, not even a Power Plant was needed. Same for RPG Launcher (and Spy Sat/Cloning Vats, this one was in a brief testing session in normal mode however)
Capturing an allied baracks grants access to basic allied infantry. With GIs already unlocked as Soviets, this resulted in two different kinds of GI in the build menu. Without price or veterancy upgrades there is no way of knowing which one is which. Probably not that important, but maybe not intended.
Not a bug but maybe an oversight: Soviets build planes like Harpies in the Warfactory, which is what I would expect. But how can they restock ammo without a helipad? Same would be the case for Yuri. Allies might use Airforce HQ for refilling.
Other than that, the shop mode worked perfectly, well done!
